GQNEWS STANDARD

Editorial policy.

Accuracy and attribution

We aim to publish accurate, useful local information. Reporting is based on named sources, first-hand reporting or clearly identified documents. We credit other outlets and do not copy their work as our own.

Clear labels

News reporting, opinion, sponsored material and community notices are labelled so readers can tell what they are reading.

Corrections

When a material factual error is confirmed, we correct it promptly and add a correction note where appropriate.

Community submissions

Submission does not guarantee publication. We may edit for clarity, check claims and decline material that is unsafe, misleading, unlawful or promotional without public value.

Contact
